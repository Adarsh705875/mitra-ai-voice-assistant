Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Patel Brothers AI Assistant demo handles information when customers interact with the service through WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

The assistant may process information provided by customers during a conversation, including:

  • WhatsApp phone number
  • Customer name, when available
  • Messages sent to the assistant
  • Membership information provided for verification
  • Pickup order information
  • Store and pickup preferences

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide customer support and demonstrate features such as:

  • Finding nearby Patel Brothers stores
  • Providing store hours and information
  • Providing information about promotions and deals
  • Searching products and prices
  • Verifying membership information
  • Creating and managing pickup orders
  • Connecting customers with human support when requested

3. Information Sharing

Customer information is not sold or rented for advertising purposes. Information may be processed by services required to operate the demonstration, including WhatsApp/Meta and connected application services.

4. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed through the assistant. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

Information may be retained for operational, testing, demonstration, troubleshooting, or customer-support purposes for the duration necessary for those purposes.

6. User Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their interaction with the assistant by contacting the service operator using the contact information provided below.

7. Third-Party Services

The assistant may use third-party services and APIs to provide messaging, automation, artificial intelligence, and data-storage functionality. Those services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

This service is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of operating this service.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
